# Technical Reports & Spatial Strategies

### UN-Habitat Technical Reports & Spatial Assessments
**United Nations Human Settlements Programme (UN-Habitat)** | *2019 – 2026*

*Contributing Author & Lead Spatial Data Analyst*

- **Sahab City-Wide Public Space Assessment (Jordan, 2026):** Developed city-wide spatial indicators and diagnostic action plans for municipal public space optimization and infrastructure planning.  
  [Read Publication](https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2026/04/260423-cwa_sahab.pdf)

- **Naryn Resilience Compendium & Spatial Strategy (Kyrgyzstan, 2025):** Led urban resilience profiling, hazard analysis, and strategic response planning with comprehensive geospatial modeling.  
  [Read Publication](https://unhabitat.org/sites/default/files/2025/01/naryn_compendium_part_1-min_1.pdf)

- **Borodianka, Makarivska and Myrhorod Recovery Frameworks (Ukraine, 2024–2025):** Engineered spatial data architecture and geospatial diagnostic modeling for war-affected municipalities. *Developed by UN-Habitat Ukraine Urban Lab in collaboration with UNITAC/City Science Lab*.

- **Khorog Integrated Spatial Plan for Environmental & Socio-Economic Resilience (Tajikistan, 2023):** Formulated GIS data integration and spatial resilience strategies for mountain hazard mitigation and sustainable urban development.  
  [Read Publication](https://unhabitat.org/integrated-spatial-plan-for-environmental-and-socio-economic-resilience-khorog-tajikistan)

- **Latin America City-Wide Public Space Assessment Series (Dominican Republic, Monteria, Colombia, Montevideo, Uruguay, 2021–2022):** Co-authored city technical reports and a regional synthesis document defining spatial public space strategies. *Developed in collaboration with the Inter-American Development Bank (IDB) and UN-Habitat Global Public Space Programme*.

- **Vision, Scenario Building, and Action Plan for Al-Hashmi Al-Janoubi Neighbourhood (Jordan, 2023):** Formulated spatial vision and scenario modeling for neighbourhood-level strategic urban planning in Al-Hashmi Al-Janoubi. *In collaboration with UPIMC (University of Petra Innovation and Modernity Centre) and the United Nations in Jordan*.  
  [Read Publication](https://jordan.un.org/sites/default/files/remote-resources/92050ed6576a421bb74b7a19f9fefa0f.pdf)

- **Semarang Spatial Profile & Capital Investment Planning (Indonesia, 2022):** Engineered territorial analytics and spatial diagnostics for infrastructure prioritization and capital investment strategies.  
  [Read Publication](https://jordan.un.org/sites/default/files/remote-resources/92050ed6576a421bb74b7a19f9fefa0f.pdf)

- **Saudi Cities Profile Series (Saudi Arabia, 2019):** Co-authored spatial analytics, GIS mapping, and territorial profiling for the *Future Saudi Cities Programme* across 10+ major Saudi municipalities. *Developed in collaboration with MoMRA (Ministry of Municipal and Rural Affairs)*.

### Academic Research & Dissertations

### MSc Dissertation: Community Leadership & Urban Development Governance in Informal Settlements
**London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE)** | *2017*

*Focus: Huaycán, Lima, Peru*

- **Qualitative Governance Analysis:** Evaluated the historic evolution of urban development and territorial consolidation in the self-managed settlement of Huaycán through qualitative research methods.
- **Stakeholder Fieldwork:** Conducted in-depth semi-structured interviews with key community leaders and local organization representatives to analyze community-driven urban planning, leadership strategies, and state-community negotiations.


### Undergraduate Thesis: Environmental Perception & Child Geography in Vulnerable Contexts
**Pontifical Catholic University of Peru (PUCP)** | *2013*

*Focus: Belén District, Maynas, Loreto (Peruvian Amazon)*

- **Spatial Perception & Fieldwork:** Investigated environmental perception and everyday geographic practices of children (ages 7–12) in the flood-prone informal settlement of Pueblo Libre (Belén, Maynas).
- **Applied Methodology:** Applied participatory spatial mapping, qualitative field assessments, and environmental psychology frameworks in high-vulnerability Amazonian environments.



### Applied University Research Project: Involuntary Resettlement & Territorial Impact
**Pontifical Catholic University of Peru (PUCP)** | *2012 – 2013*

*Project: "Una ciudad que despega, un pueblo que desaparece: el caso del reasentamiento involuntario de la población del A.H. El Ayllu"*

- **Urban Displacement Analysis:** Co-investigated the spatial, social, and territorial impacts of airport expansion infrastructure leading to the involuntary displacement and resettlement of the *El Ayllu* community.
